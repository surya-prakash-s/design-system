## 2025-02-27 - Context-Aware Screen Reader Labels in Pill Lists
**Learning:** For multi-item list components like Pills, having static fallback titles or assistive text (e.g., "Remove") on action buttons fails WCAG 2.1 accessibility guidelines, as screen-reader users lose visual association and cannot identify which specific item is being removed. Consistently propagating the component's unique label into the action element's accessibility attributes (e.g., "Remove [Pill Label]") ensures standard-compliant, descriptive screen reader navigation.
**Action:** Always design multi-item actions (delete, edit, remove buttons inside lists, tables, or pill arrays) to dynamically incorporate their item's unique label or key identifier into both 'title' and screen-reader 'assistiveText' properties.
